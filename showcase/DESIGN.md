# LEVEL visual system

Experience mode: hardware launch studio, grounded in the tactile rituals of a gaming desk. A large dimensional keyboard leads into gear exploration, a playable reaction experiment, and a saved personal setup.

Tokens: ivory #f1f0eb, ink #252620, muted olive #73766b, electric orange #f4512a. Theme swatches also allow sage, lilac, and cyan. Space Grotesk typography. Display 38–96px, body 12–14px, detail 10–12px. Spacing uses 4/8/12/16/24/32px with generous 55–85px section separation. Small radii on controls; circular utility targets. Photography stays rectangular and editorial.

Motion centers on the dimensional keyboard and sequenced lighting. Respect reduced motion. Dialogs manage focus and Escape. Mobile stacks the hero, retains the full playable keyboard, and uses a two-column collection.
